Can the botnet pattern live in the network?

Pattern H = a hub with 4 leaves (star K1,4). We search the network G for copies of H (subgraph isomorphism), cut edges so none remain, then test whether an attacker with a budget of k new edges can rebuild one.

Result. G contains K1,4 iff some node has degree ≥ 4, and the number of copies is Σ C(deg(v),4). An attacker needs 4 − Δ(G′) new edges to rebuild one, so to stop budget k the defender must cap Δ at 3 − k. Capping at 3 alone leaves the attacker a cost of just 1.
